Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

news-channel-dan-ng is used only by its owner, Dan Ng, with his own TikTok account.

  • Data received from TikTok: the access token, refresh token and open ID of the owner's account, and the upload status of each video.
  • How it is used: only to upload the owner's videos to his TikTok drafts.
  • Storage: tokens are kept in a local file on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services), and deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
